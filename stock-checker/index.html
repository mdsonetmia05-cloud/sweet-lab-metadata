2 · Submitting a vector?

The checker above measures raster images (JPG / PNG). It cannot verify a vector file.

Adobe Stock vector rule: minimum 15 megapixel artboard.
For vector artwork, Adobe requires an artboard of at least 15 megapixels — for example 5000 × 3000 px (15 MP). A raster check cannot verify a vector artboard, so check your artboard size in your vector app (for example Adobe Illustrator: File → Document Setup, or check the artboard dimensions) and make sure your EPS / AI file meets the 15 MP minimum before submitting. A JPG preview exported from the vector does not prove the artboard size.
